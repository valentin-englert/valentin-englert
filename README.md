# `whoami`

I just want to turn my ideas into something real. That often leads me into unexpected niches – or into picking up skills that were never part of the plan.

> **Small curiosities have a habit of turning into projects.**

Software, research, lab work, 3D printing, design and craft are all parts of my toolbox. If I don't know how to do something yet, I'll usually try to figure it out.

What I enjoy most is watching a vague idea slowly take shape and eventually work the way I imagined it. Getting there isn't always fun ;)

**How I build** – AI agents let me build ideas that had been sitting in my head for years. They write code, tests and documentation, and help with research. I set the direction, review the results and decide what ships.
